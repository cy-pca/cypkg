cypkg

A client for cvcpkg registries: search packages, read their builds and recipes, and watch the builders live. It is written as Ariadne documents, so the same app runs in each of these. Pick one.

  • Graphical arigl Windows you drag, resize and click, drawn with Dear ImGui on WebGL. Mouse first. about 16 MB to download
  • Terminal ari · FTXUI The terminal app, in an xterm.js terminal. Keyboard first: Tab between fields, Enter, F10 for the menu. about 9 MB to download
  • Terminal ari · tvision The same app on a Turbo Vision desktop: overlapping windows you drag by their titles, F6 for the next one. about 9 MB to download

Each one runs WebAssembly with JSPI: Chrome or Edge 137, Firefox 153 or Safari 27, or later.

In your own terminal: cvcpkg install cypkg, then cypkg. Source: cy-pca/cypkg.

The runners are libcvc's, under the LGPL-2.1, with third-party notices.
